* _15_ of our alumni are now on the GMU CS faculty (totally surprised!!)


Finally, we need your help!!! This 2 day old database is obviously still
in its infancy and undoubtedly misses many students and contains
information that is incomplete or inaccurate.

Contributing should be *very* simple (no need to edit data or code).
Please send whatever is easiest for you, e.g., email, Slack message,
GitHub issue, submission through the website, a link to your student
list, or simply a student's name and a few details (e.g., best paper
awards at CHI'22). Suggestions for improving the UI, search, or
insights/stats are also very welcome.

Even small contributions will help us build a much more accurate and
useful record of our students' accomplishments.

Thanks!
